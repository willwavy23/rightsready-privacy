Privacy Policy

RightsReady  |  Effective date: March 25, 2026

RightsReady is designed with your privacy as a default. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

No Personal Data Collected

RightsReady does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any account credentials.

No Tracking or Analytics

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not monitor how you use the app.

Data Stored on Your Device Only

Any data you create within RightsReady — including:

  • Bookmarked rights and legal references
  • Incident logs you record
  • App preferences and settings

— is saved exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Offline First

Core app functionality works entirely offline. No internet connection is required to access rights information, bookmarks, or incident logs.

Optional AI Search

RightsReady includes an optional AI-powered search feature. If you use this feature, your search query is sent to the Anthropic API to generate a response. No other information is included with that request. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle API inputs.

This feature can be ignored or left unused — it is not required for any core functionality.

Children's Privacy

RightsReady does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, COPPA obligations do not apply.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Reach out via the app's support page or the contact information listed in the App Store.
