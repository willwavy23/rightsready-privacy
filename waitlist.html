Questions

Frequently asked

Still not sure? Here's what most people want to know.

RightsReady is a mobile app designed to give you clear, actionable guidance on your legal rights, emergency procedures, and important personal information — all available offline and organized for high-stress moments. Whether you're being pulled over, dealing with a landlord dispute, or responding to an emergency, RightsReady helps you stay prepared.

RightsReady is currently in final development. We're preparing for an early access launch and are accepting waitlist signups now. People on the waitlist will be the first to receive download access when we go live.

Waitlist members get first access when the app launches, occasional product updates, and the opportunity to provide feedback that directly shapes the app's features. We keep email communication minimal — you'll only hear from us when it matters.

RightsReady's core content — rights guides, emergency information, offline translator, and document vault — is available for free. A Pro tier will unlock advanced features. Early access users will receive extended free access to Pro features as a thank-you.

RightsReady is being built for iOS and Android simultaneously. Both platforms will be supported at launch. A web version may follow post-launch.
